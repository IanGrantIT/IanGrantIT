Ian Grant

Ian Grant is a Dallas-based glitch artist, punk rocker, and maker working with analog video, photography, and broken machines. He uses old hardware, messed up signals, feedback, and distortion to make images fall apart and turn into something new. To him, destroying a system can create some of the best results.

"The passion for destruction is also a creative passion!" Russian anarchist Mikhail Bakunin, 1842.

Glitch is kind of like painting with broken technology. The work is about trying to control chaos, but not too much. The mistakes, noise, texture, and failures are the point. A lot of the process comes from painting, photography, internet culture, video art, punk music, and being interested in what happens when you force a machine to do something it was never supposed to do.

Ian is not really a fan of the art world, but he is a fan of artists. He would rather walk through an abandoned building looking at graffiti or drink a 40 at a backyard punk show than stand around in an art museum. For him, art is not just something you make and put on a wall. It is a lifestyle, a way to express himself, and a way to connect with other people. He believes there is so much more creativity in the art of telling stories around a fire, embracing modern folklore, and traveling than people think. He cares about supporting his community and wants to eventually open his own gallery — a place for artists and people who usually get ignored by the “art world” to actually have a stage.

He makes videos, prints, music videos, audiovisual work, and digital pieces. Hit him up for commissions.

The future is unwritten.
