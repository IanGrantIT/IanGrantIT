Ian Grant is a glitch artist working in analog video and photography, based in Dallas, Texas. He arrived at glitch art after studying painting and photography, and from being active on the internet. To him glitch art is like painting with broken systems and also feels influenced by video art. He uses intentionally sabotaged hardware to manipulate and create audiovisuals. The pieces hopefully allow the viewer to have a connection with the process and the materiality of the medium. His interest is in attempting to control the inherent chaos of glitch and to create novel sensory experiences. Hopefully the viewer finds something interesting and new about this process. I make videos, prints, music videos and NFTs. Feel free to contact me for commissions.
